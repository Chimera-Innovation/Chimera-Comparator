# Mallard-Avenue Season Story

This story uses human-observed Paint markings only. Raw NDVI is included as context and background, not as a detector.

## May 12

Early concern first observed at 16.57% human-marked vigour-loss coverage.

## May 18

Marked coverage decreased to 13.11%, while remaining human-observed areas still require review.

## May 22

Marked coverage decreased to 9.22%, while remaining human-observed areas still require review.

## May 29

Additional concern zones emerged or expanded, reaching 25.34% coverage.

## Summary

Concern started in the lower-field area.

Concern persisted most clearly in the lower-field area.

Concern expanded most clearly in the lower-field area.

Next season, scouting should prioritize the repeated human-observed concern zones before diagnosing cause. Review irrigation uniformity, drainage, fertility, disease pressure, crop history, and field operations.
